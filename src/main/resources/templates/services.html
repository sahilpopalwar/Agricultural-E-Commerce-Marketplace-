Frequently Asked Questions

What areas do you deliver to?

We currently deliver to all major cities in India with plans to expand. Enter your pin code during checkout to check availability in your area. Our 2-hour express delivery is available in select metropolitan areas.

How do I know your products are truly organic?

All our organic products come with certification from recognized authorities like India Organic or USDA (for imported items). We also provide detailed farm information and growing practices for complete transparency.

Can I customize my subscription box?

Absolutely! Our subscription service is fully customizable. You can choose specific items, set preferences for what you don't want, and adjust quantities. You can also pause or modify your subscription anytime.

What if I receive damaged or spoiled items?

We stand by our 100% satisfaction guarantee. Simply contact our support team within 24 hours with photos of the issue, and we'll either replace the items or refund you immediately - no questions asked.
